# Properties

### Warm
You are resistant to <span style="color:cyan">cold</span> damage from the environment.

### Flexible
Your armor is flexible, granting you resistance to <span style="color:lightslategrey">bludgeoning</span> damage.

### Unbreakable x
Damage that you take is reduced by x, except for <span style="color:red">force</span> and <span style="color:hotpink">psychic</span> damage.

### Force protection
You have advantage on saving throws against Force powers.

### Cold fibers
You are invisible to thermal devices.

### Beskar
You are resistant to <span style="color:gold">radiant</span> damage.

### Disruptive
You are resistant to damage from lightsabers, and a lightsaber that hits you becomes disrupted and can't attack until the start of its wielder's next turn.

### Fragile
The composition of your armor is weak to explosions. If you get hit by an explosion, roll a d20: on a 3 or less, the armor breaks and you no longer benefit from it.

### Complete cover
Your armor protects you from various dangers. While wearing the full armor, you gain resistance to <span style="color:lawngreen">poison</span> and <span style="color:greenyellow">acid</span> damage.

### Resistant
Your armor protects you from blades and spikes. While wearing the full armor, you gain resistance to <span style="color:lightslategrey">slashing</span> and <span style="color:lightslategrey">piercing</span> damage.

### Hands free
This shield is attached to your arm, keeping your hand free.

### Ionic shield x - 1dy
A ionic shield take more easily the shots but have a limited capacity.<br>
x represent the maximal Hit Points of the shield. Every time you get hit by an attack roll, the shield takes the damages. If the damages exceed the shield remaining Hit Points, you still get hit but the damages are reduced by the Hit Points the shield had.<br>
At the beginning of your turn, the shield gain 1dy Hit Points.<br>
Ionic shields are luminous, thus giving you disadvantage on Stealth checks, but you can turn them off.

### Pressurized
The armor protects you from gas and the vacuum of space. You can breathe in the vacuum of space for 10 minutes, recharging when entering a breathable area.
You are immune to poison.
