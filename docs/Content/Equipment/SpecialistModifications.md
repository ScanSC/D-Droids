# Specialist Modifications

### Ionic Shield
**Armor part**: Vambrace<br>
**Description**: Add a hands-free [Small Ionic Shield](../Equipment/Armors.md).<br>
**Improved**: Add your Charisma modifier to the shield's Hit Point regeneration.

### Whistling Bird
**Armor part**: Vambrace<br>
**Description**: A Whistling Bird is a type of weapon used by Mandalorians. These small guided munitions are placed in Mandalorian vambraces and, when deployed, fly through the air with a whistling noise before hitting the target with a small explosion.<br>
Using an action, you can shoot a number of projectiles equal to your proficiency bonus at any number of targets within 30 feet. Each projectile deals 1d8 + your gadget ability modifier <span style="color:lightslategrey">piercing</span> + 1d8 <span style="color:darkorange">fire</span> damage.<br>
The Whistling Bird has 12 rounds of ammunition. Using Tinker's tools during a long rest, you can craft 2d4 rounds of ammunition; on a failed check, you craft half that amount.<br>
**Improved**: The <span style="color:lightslategrey">piercing</span> damage becomes 3d4 instead of 1d8.

### Electric Darts
**Armor part**: Vambrace<br>
**Description**: Using 1 Gadget Point and a bonus action, you shoot a small dart at a creature within 60 feet (make a ranged attack roll). The creature can use an action to remove and break the dart. A creature can have only one dart stuck in it.<br>
Using an action, you can trigger the darts. Each creature within 10 feet of a dart takes 1d6 <span style="color:dodgerblue">lightning</span> damage and loses its reaction. The damage is cumulative.
**Improved**: You can shoot two darts at once.

### Grappling Hook

**Improved**: Increase its range by 10 feet.

### Dorsal Rocket
**Armor part**: Jetpack or backpack<br>
**Description**: Add a hands-free [Rocket Launcher](../Equipment/Equipments.md) with a cost of 0 Gadget Points, but it can be used only once until you find, craft, or buy a new rocket.<br>
**Improved**: Increase its range by 60 feet and its DC by your Charisma modifier.

### 3rd Arm
**Armor part**: Jetpack or backpack<br>
**Description**: Add a small robotic arm able to hold a handgun or other small objects, such as a flashlight. If the arm holds a handgun, you can fire it using a bonus action, and it can use either your Dexterity or Intelligence modifier for the attack.<br>
**Improved**: The arm can shoot twice using the same bonus action.

### Aim Assist
**Armor part**: A visor helmet<br>
**Description**: Add an aim assist inside your helmet, increasing any ranged attack roll by 1.<br>
**Improved**: The bonus increases to 2.
