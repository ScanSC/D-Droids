# Feats

Name                 | Prerequisites                   | Description
---------------------|---------------------------------|------------
Darts champion       | -                               | Always succeed on a stim throw to an ally up to 20 ft, and can throw a stim up to 10 additional feet.
O Blood Type         | Constitution >= 12              | All healing you receive is increased by your Constitution modifier.
Drug addict          | -                               | All stims you use on yourself cost one less Gadget Point (minimum of 1), but every time a stim is injected in you, your hit point maximum decreases by 3. Your hit points return to normal after a long rest.
Weak immune system   | Not having Strong immune system | Every effect with a duration affecting your body last for one more turn.
Strong immune system | Not having Weak immune system   | Every effect with a duration affecting your body last for one less turn (min 1).
Additional training  | -                               | Choose a new fighting style
Lightsaber master    | Lightsaber proficiency          | When you succeed on a parry with a lightsaber, you return the attack on an 18, 19, or 20.
Executor             | Perception proficiency          | When a creature survives damage within your attack range and has 5 Hit Points or fewer remaining, you can use your reaction to make an attack against it.
Armor Tweak          | Not having Specialist subclass  | You learn two Armor Modifications from the [Specialist Modifications](../Equipment/SpecialistModifications.md) following the [Armor modifications](../SubClasses/Specialist.md) feature rules.
Ionic Protections    | -                               | +1 in Strength, Dexterity or Constitution. You gain proficiency with Ionic Shields, Deployable Barriers and Ionic Domes.

For other DnD feats you can take the ones not about magic or races.
