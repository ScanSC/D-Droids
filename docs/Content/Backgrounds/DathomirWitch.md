# Background: Dathomir Witch
*You are a true Dathomir Witch of Dathomir, but something drove you to leave your home far behind. You are not a NightSister*

**Skill Proficiencies**: Nature, Arcane\
**Tool Proficiencies**: -\
**Languages**: Dathomiri\
**Equipment**: A trinket, a set of Dathomiri clothes, and a pouch containing 15 credits

### Magicks
You learn two Force Tricks from the special [Allyan Magic table](../SubClasses/Dathomir.md).
You have a number of Force Dies equal to your proficiency bonus.
See the [Force Trick](../Classes/ForceSensitive.md) feature for details about their use.
