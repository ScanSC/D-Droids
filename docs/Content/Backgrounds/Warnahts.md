# Background: Warnahts
*You’ve certainly made some questionable choices in your life; the result is that you are now bound to a Nightsister who grants you powers, but at what cost?*

**Skill Proficiencies**: Intimidation, Arcane<br>
**Tool Proficiencies**: -<br>
**Languages**: Dathomiri<br>
**Equipment**: A cursed trinket gifted by the Nightsister, a set of common clothes, and a pouch containing 10 credits

### Cursed powers
You learn two Force Tricks from the special [Night Spells table](../SubClasses/Dathomir.md).
You have a number of Force Dies equal to your proficiency bonus.
See the [Force Trick](../Classes/ForceSensitive.md) feature for details about their use.
