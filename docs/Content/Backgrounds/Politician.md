# Background: Politician
*You rose through the political ranks of your people. But how did you end up there?*

**Skill Proficiencies**: Representation, Deception\
**Tool Proficiencies**: -\
**Languages**: Two of your choice\
**Equipment**: Papers proving your position, a memento from your home, a set of magnificent clothes, and a pouch containing 40 credits

### Connexions
You are respected by the representatives of your people, provided that they respect their politicians.\
However, the doors to your planet and its territories will always remain open to you, and you will be able to find protection and supplies there. That might also be the case for your allies.
