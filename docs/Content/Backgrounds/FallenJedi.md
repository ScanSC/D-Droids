# Background: Fallen Jedi
*Whether it was your decision or theirs, you are no longer part of the Jedi Order. You left and kept your precious lightsaber.*

**Skill Proficiencies**: Arcane, Survival\
**Tool Proficiencies**: Any lightsaber\
**Languages**: -\
**Equipment**: Your lightsaber, a set of used Jedi clothes, and a pouch containing 20 credits

### May the Force (still) be with you
This radical change in your life has disrupted your connection to the Force, but you still retain some abilities.\
You gain the [Force Power](../Classes/ForceSensitive.md) feature from the Force Sensitive class.
