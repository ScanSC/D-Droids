# Bounty Hunter: Trapper

Your equipment uses your Wisdom modifier.

## Improvised trap
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you learn to construct a trap from a grenade. Using Tinker's tools and a grenade, you can place a trap (wire trap, mine, etc.).<br>
When triggered, the trap creates the grenade's effects.<br>
You are proficient with Tinkering Tools.

## Discreet assembly
At 6th level, your traps become less visible. Enemies have a penalty on their Perception checks to detect them equal to your Intelligence modifier.

## Sabotage
At 9th level, you become able to sabotage some machines.<br>
Using Tinker's tools, you can target a machine within 5 feet and make a Sleight of Hand check (the DC depends on the target). On a success, choose one of the following two effects:
- Deactivates the machine
- The next time a creature tries to interact with it, it takes a number of d8 <span style="color:dodgerblue">lightning</span> damage equal to your Intelligence modifier. 

## Electric Cage
At 11th level, you created a unique trap.<br>
Using an action, you place it in a 5ft range from you.<br>
When a creature steps on it, the cage unfolds and traps the creature, dealing it 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier <span style="color:dodgerblue">lightning</span> damage and making it drop any weapon in its hands.<br>
The cage stays active for a number of hours equal to your Intelligence modifier.<br>
A creature trying to touch the cage takes 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier <span style="color:dodgerblue">lightning</span> damage.<br>
The cage can be destroyed by shooting precisely on the system.

## Rapid Triggering
At 17th level, the DC of your traps is increased by 1d4.
