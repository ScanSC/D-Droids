# Voyager: Pirate

Your equipment uses your Charisma modifier.

## Strategic Retreat
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain special abilities when piloting a spaceship.<br>
If the spaceship didn't attack since your last turn, its AC increases by your Charisma modifier.<br>
Moreover, you need one less turn to enter hyperspace.

## Loathsome opponent
At 3rd level, you can use your bonus action to insult someone within 30 feet. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take <span style="color:hotpink">psychic</span> damage equal to your Charisma modifier and have disadvantage on its next attack roll.

## Throat or Treats
At 9th level, you have advantage on Intimidation checks to obtain a good deal.

## Rakish Audacity
At 13th level, your confidence propels you into battle. You can give yourself a bonus to your initiative rolls equal to your Charisma modifier.

You also gain an additional way to use your Sneak Attack; you don't need advantage on the attack roll to use your Sneak Attack against a creature if you are within 5 feet of it, no other creatures are within 5 feet of you, and you don't have disadvantage on the attack roll. All the other rules for Sneak Attack still apply to you.

## Master duelist
At 17th level, your mastery of the combat lets you turn failure into success. If you miss with an attack roll, you can roll it again with advantage.<br>
Once you do so, you can't use this feature again until you finish a short or long rest.
