# Voyager: Phantom

Your equipment uses your Charisma modifier.

## Fast Hands
Starting at 3rd level, you can use the bonus action granted by your Cunning Action to make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check, use your Hacking Tool to disarm a trap or open a lock, or take the Use an Object action.\
Moreover, you have advantage on ability checks to unlock a door or chest.

## In the Shadows
At 9th level, you become proficient with [Infiltrator Suit](../Equipment/Armors.md).\
Moreover, your footsteps don't make a sound while you are not wearing medium or heavy armor.

## Second-Story Work
At 13th level, you gain the ability to climb faster than normal; climbing no longer costs you extra movement.

In addition, when you make a running jump, the distance you cover increases by a number of feet equal to your Dexterity modifier.

## Thief's Reflexes
At 17th level, you have become adept at laying ambushes and quickly escaping danger. You can take two turns during the first round of any combat. You take your first turn at your normal initiative and your second turn at your initiative minus 10. You can't use this feature when you are surprised.
