# Bounty Hunter: Poisoner

Your equipment uses your Intelligence modifier.

## Poisoner kit
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with stim pistols.<br>
Your healing stim and regenerating stim deal <span style="color:lawngreen">poison</span> or <span style="color:greenyellow">acid</span> damage (choose when you use it) instead of healing.<br>
Moreover, your gas grenades can deal <span style="color:greenyellow">acid</span> damage instead of <span style="color:lawngreen">poison</span> damage and use your Intelligence modifier.

## Reversed metabolism
At 3rd level, your countless experiments on yourself with poisons and chemicals have modified your metabolism. Healing effects deal damage to you, but <span style="color:lawngreen">poison</span> damage heals you. You are also resistant to <span style="color:greenyellow">acid</span> and immune to poisoning.

## Side Effects
At 6th level, when a creature is affected by one of your poisoned stims, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become poisoned until the end of its next turn.

## Rabbit Hole
At 9th level, you’ve read and watched everything there is to see about poisons and venomous creatures.<br>
You know if a creature is venomous and you have advantage on Animal Handling checks with them.<br>
Moreover, your <span style="color:lawngreen">poison</span> and <span style="color:greenyellow">acid</span> damage ignore resistances.

## Arsenic pudding
The thing you love about poisons is that when you mix together just about any dangerous substances, the effect is always brutal.<br>
At 11th level, all your <span style="color:lawngreen">poison</span> and <span style="color:greenyellow">acid</span> damage is increased by your Intelligence modifier.

## Ultimate Poison
At 17th level, your <span style="color:lawngreen">poison</span> damage ignores immunity, and any poisoning effect you inflict lasts for one more turn.
