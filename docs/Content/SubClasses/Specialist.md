# Bounty Hunter: Specialist

Your equipment uses your Charisma modifier.

## Polyvalent arsenal
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with precision rifles, binoculars, hand flamethrowers, Mandalorian vambraces, and jetpacks.\
Moreover you have two use of the jetpack between two short rests.

## Mandalorian vambrace
At 3rd level, you obtain a [mandalorian vambraces](../Equipment/Armors.md).
Choose one equipment, weapon or shield among the following list and integrates it to one of you vambrace. You become proficient with the integrated device.\
Integrated equipment only needs a bonus action to activate (except for melee weapons).
Integrated equipment can have a benefit or a penalty compared to when it is not integrated.

Integrated Equipment                            | Modifier
------------------------------------------------|--------
[Small Ionic Shield](../Equipment/Armors.md)    | Hand-free
[Whistling Bird](../Equipment/Equipments.md)    | -
[Hand Flamethrower](../Equipment/Equipments.md) | Reduce cost by 1gp
[Grappling Boa](../Equipment/Weapons.md)        | Hand-free
[Hidden blade](../Equipment/Weapons.md)         | +1d4 damage
[Rocket Launcher](../Equipment/Equipments.md)   | Radius reduced by 10ft, Range reduced by 100ft
[Ionic Blaster](../Equipment/Weapons.md)        | Damage reduced to 1d4
[Taser](../Equipment/Equipments.md)             | -

At 9th level, you can integrate new equipment into the second vambrace.

## Jetpack Maneuver
At 6th level, you learn one jetpack maneuver among the list below.
Moreover you gain a third use of the jetpack between two short rests.

Maneuver          | Requirement to use it                        | Description
------------------|----------------------------------------------|------------
Reactor Strike    | 12 Strength                                  | After flying for at least 10 feet, if you directly make a melee attack, the damage is increased by 2d6.
Scorching landing |                                              | After flying with your jetpack, you can choose to land (losing all the remaining flying speed) and burst fire in a 5-foot radius. All creatures (except you) in this area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or receive [Burning](../Conditions.md) for one turn.
Flood of fire     | A range weapon without the surgical property | You can use your action and one use of your jetpack to rise and to rain down fire in a 10-foot radius. Roll your weapon damage one time. Each creature in a 10-foot radius must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes the full damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The damage with a burst weapon a rolled one time. The weapon overheat increase by the maximal amount of its cooling action.
Brutal landing    | 12 Constitution                              | After flying with your jetpack, you can choose to land (losing all the remaining flying speed) and burst fire in a 5-foot radius. All creatures (except you) in this area must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be knocked prone.
Reflex Dodge      | 12 Dexterity                                 | If you are in the area of an explosion or other similar effects, you can use your reaction and one use of your jetpack to fly out from the danger. You only move to the closest safe area.
Clearance         |                                              | You can use a use of your jetpack to give you +5 to your next saving throw against any restraining condition.

## Last resort
At 17th level, your modify your jetpack to integrate a missile inside.\
One time per long rest, you can use your action to shoot the missile with the same properties as the rocket launcher.\
Moreover you gain expertise in on skill you are proficient with.
