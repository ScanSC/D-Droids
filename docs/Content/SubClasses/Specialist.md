# Bounty Hunter: Specialist

Your equipment uses your Charisma modifier.

## Phoenix training
At 3rd level, you gain proficiency with precision rifles, Mandalorian vambraces, and jetpacks, and you have two uses of the jetpack between two short rests.

## Armor modifications
At 3rd level, you learn to modify your armor to integrate specialized equipment into it. During a long rest, you can integrate a number of pieces of equipment equal to your Intelligence modifier, provided you have the equipment. You can also swap two pieces of equipment.<br>
Each part of your armor can hold one modification at a time, and you can have a total number of modifications equal to your proficiency bonus.<br>
You are proficient with any integrated device.<br>
Integrated equipment only needs a bonus action to activate (except for melee weapons).<br>
Integrated equipment can have a benefit or a penalty compared to when it is not integrated.

You know a number of Modifications equal to your proficiency bonus. Whenever this number increases, choose a new Modification from the [Specialist Modifications](../Equipment/SpecialistModifications.md) list.

## Jetpack Maneuver
At 6th level, you learn one jetpack maneuver among the list below.
Moreover you gain a third use of the jetpack between two short rests.

Maneuver          | Requirement to use it                        | Description
------------------|----------------------------------------------|------------
Reactor Strike    | 12 Strength                                  | After flying for at least 10 feet, if you directly make a melee attack, the damage is increased by 2d6.
Scorching landing |                                              | After flying with your jetpack, you can choose to land (losing all the remaining flying speed) and burst fire in a 5-foot radius. All creatures (except you) in this area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or receive [Burning](../Conditions.md) for one turn.
Flood of fire     | A ranged weapon without the Surgical property | You can use your action and one use of your jetpack to rise and rain down fire in a 10-foot radius. Roll your weapon's damage once. Each creature in a 10-foot radius must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes the full damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The damage from a burst weapon is rolled once. The weapon's overheat increases by the maximum amount of its cooling action.
Brutal landing    | 12 Constitution                              | After flying with your jetpack, you can choose to land (losing all the remaining flying speed) and burst fire in a 5-foot radius. All creatures (except you) in this area must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be knocked prone.
Reflex Dodge      | 12 Dexterity                                 | If you are in the area of an explosion or other similar effects, you can use your reaction and one use of your jetpack to fly out from the danger. You only move to the closest safe area.
Clearance         |                                              | You can use a use of your jetpack to give you +5 to your next saving throw against any restraining condition.

## Armor Expert
At 11th level, you empower your integrated devices. Your Armor Modifications now use the Improved property shown in each Modification's description.<br>

## War Machine
At 17th level, you can apply two Modifications to each armor part. Moreover, every Modification that uses Gadget Points costs 1 less (minimum 1).
