# Soldier: Body Guard

Your equipment uses your Wisdom modifier.

## Guardian
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with defibrillators, all ionic shields, heavy armors, deployable barriers, and ionic domes.\ 
Moreover when a creature in a 5ft range from you get hit by an attack and you are using a shield, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage taken according to the shield used.
Shield              | Damage reduction
--------------------|-----------------
Small shield        | 1d4 + Constitution modifier
Riot shield         | 1d6 + Constitution modifier
Small ionic shield  | 2d4 + Constitution modifier
Medium ionic shield | 2d6 + Constitution modifier
Riot ionic shield   | 2d8 + Constitution modifier
If you use this feature with an ionic shield, it uses the ionic shield's Hit Points in the same way as for yourself.

## War colossus
At 7th level, while wearing heavy armor, you have advantage on Athletics checks to grapple a creature, and you don't get a penalty for grappling a creature one size bigger than you.<br>
Moreover, if you have to carry a creature, you don't get penalty to your walking speed.<br>
Any creature in 5ft from you benefit from a half cover.

## Overclocked batteries
At 10th level, all your ionic barriers, such as ionic shield, deployable barrier or ionic dome maximum Hit Points are increased by your Wisdom modifier bonus * your proficiency bonus.

## Exceptional constitution
At 15th level, your Hit Points increase by an amount equal to your Constitution modifier bonus * your proficiency bonus.<br>
Moreover you have advantage on saving throws against something trying to push you or knock you prone.

## Impenetrable
At 18th level, any creature in 5ft from you benefit from a three quarter cover.<br>
Additionally, the Protector feature doesn't consume your reaction anymore.
