# Soldier: Air Trooper

Your equipment uses your Intelligence modifier.

## Jetpack trained
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with jetpacks.<br>
Moreover, you have three uses of the jetpack between two short rests.

## Jetpack Maneuver
At 7th level, you learn one jetpack maneuver among the list below.
Moreover you gain a fourth use of the jetpack between two short rests.

Maneuver          | Requirement to use it                        | Description
------------------|----------------------------------------------|------------
Reactor Strike    | 12 Strength                                  | After flying for at least 10 ft, if you directly make a melee attack, the damage is increased by 2d6.
Scorching landing |                                              | After flying with your jetpack, you can choose to land (losing all the remaining flying speed) and burst fire in a 5-foot radius. All creatures (except you) in this area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or receive [Burning](../Conditions.md) for one turn.
Flood of fire     | A range weapon without the surgical property | You can use your action and one use of your jetpack to rise and to rain down fire in a 10-foot radius. Roll your weapon damage one time. Each creature in a 10-foot radius must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes the full damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The damage with a burst weapon a rolled one time. The weapon overheat increase by the maximal amount of its cooling action.
Brutal landing    | 12 Constitution                              | After flying with your jetpack, you can choose to land (losing all the remaining flying speed) and burst fire in a 5-foot radius. All creatures (except you) in this area must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be knocked prone.
Reflex Dodge      | 12 Dexterity                                 | If you are in the area of an explosion or other similar effects, you can use your reaction and one use of your jetpack to fly out from the danger. You only move to the closest safe area.
Clearance         |                                              | You can use a use of your jetpack to give you +5 to your next saving throw against any restraining condition.

## Jetpack master
At 10th level, you increase your jetpack flying speed by 20ft.
Moreover, you gain a fifth use of the jetpack between two short rests and learn a second maneuver.

## Faster than light
At 15th level you are so fast with your jetpack, enemies do not have time to react. When flying with your jetpack you always dodge overwatch and opportunity attacks if they can miss.<br>
Moreover you gain a sixth use of the jetpack and dont need a bonus action anymore to fly.<br>
You learn a third maneuver.

## Is it a bird? Is it a plane? No. It's an air trooper
At 18th level, you increase your jetpack flying speed by 20 feet, have ten uses of the jetpack, and learn a fourth maneuver.
