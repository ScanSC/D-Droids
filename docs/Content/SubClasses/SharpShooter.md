# Soldier: Sharpshooter

Your equipment uses your Wisdom modifier.

## Uncanny eye
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with all precision rifles.<br>
Moreover your ranged attacks ignore the enemy half cover.

## Steady Aim
At 3rd level, as a bonus action, you give yourself a bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier on your next attack roll on the current turn. You can use this bonus action only if you haven't moved during this turn, and after you use the bonus action, your speed is 0 until the end of the current turn.

## Patient Shot
At 7th level, when you take your time, you never miss. If you miss, that means you didn't take enough time. When attacking with any precision rifle, you can consume any remaining extra attacks to empower your current attack with +5 to hit and one more damage die for each consumed extra attack.

## Calculated shot
At 10th level, you can shoot with your range weapons at their max range without disadvantage.<br>
This also improves the range of your overwatch attacks.

## Overcalculated shot
At 15th level, the increasingly difficult shots have shaped your brain and your instinct.<br>
Through your lens, your eyes discern the geometry of the world—every straight line, every angle, every mathematical theorem you were convinced you had forgotten.<br>
Whenever you use a precision rifle, if you know the position of a target, you can try a shot with a bounce to hit it. Your attack roll against hit will have a penalty of -10.<br>
This attack ignores directional protections such as shields and cover.

## Picky shooter
At 18th level, the question is no longer "will you hit," but "where will you hit ?".<br>
Whenever you make an attack with a precision rifle, you can aim for specific parts of the target, such as:
- its weapon to disarm it (no damage)
- its eyes to blind it
- its leg to halve its speed
You can also aim at small targets such as a remote or a light bulb without disadvantage.
