# Force Sensitive: Knight
*You learnt to make the Force of your blood work with the force of your body.*

Your equipment uses your Wisdom modifier.

## Martial training
You gain proficiency with medium common armors, all common shields, small ionic shields, common military weapons and all grenades.\
Choose one fighting style from the [Soldier](../Classes/Soldier.md) class.\

## Strengthened Force
Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.\
Moreover, the "Boomerang" trick is no more restricted to light weapons.

## Knight's Accelerated trick
At 9th level, after making an attack, you can use a Force trick as a bonus action.

## Reinforced Strength
At 15th level, whenever you make a roll using your Strength, you can consume one Force Die and add it to the result.
