# Bounty Hunter: Assassin

Your equipment uses your Wisdom modifier.

## Uncanny eye
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with any precision rifle and binoculars.<br>
Moreover, your ranged attacks ignore an enemy's half cover.

## Dread Ambusher
At 3rd level, you master the art of the ambush. You can give yourself a bonus to your initiative rolls equal to your Wisdom modifier.<br>
At the start of your first turn of each combat, your walking speed increases by 10 feet, which lasts until the end of that turn. If you take the Attack action on that turn, you can make one additional weapon attack as part of that action. If that attack hits, the target takes an extra 1d8 damage of the weapon's damage type.

## Favored enemy
At 6th level, you become good at focusing a specific target.<br>
At the start of your first turn of each combat, you designate your favored enemy.<br>
Until the end of the combat, any attack roll you do against your favored enemy deals bonus damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.

## Chained shots
At 9th level, you can use your action to make a ranged attack against creatures within 10 feet of a point you can see within your weapon’s range. You make a separate attack roll for each target.<br>
This action can target a number of creatures equal to your proficiency bonus.

## Stalker's Flurry
At 11th level, you learn to attack with such unexpected speed that you can turn a miss into another strike. Once on each of your turns when you miss with a weapon attack, you can make another weapon attack as part of the same action.

## Lethal strike
At 17th level, if you are not in combat, you can make an attack against a target who is unaware of your presence. If you hit, this attack is automatically a critical hit. You must not have disadvantage on the attack.<br>
Once you use this feature, you can't use it until a short rest.
