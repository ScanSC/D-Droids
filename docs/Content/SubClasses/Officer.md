# Soldier: Officer

Your equipment uses your Charisma modifier.

## White-collar
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with binoculars.<br>
You also gain proficiency with Persuasion.<br>

## Leader
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you become able to give instructions, advice, or encouragement to your allies, according to your management methods.<br>
As a bonus action, you can give an ally a 1d6 bonus to their next attack roll or saving throw.<br>
You can use this feature four times between each long rest.

## Dew it
You prefer giving orders to following them.<br>
At 7th level, you can use a bonus action to order an ally who can hear you to attack a target you can see. If the ally is willing, they can make one free attack against the target.<br>
You cannot give an order to an ally using overwatch or barrage fire.<br>
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus between two short rests.

## Inspiring commander
Whether you inspire fear, respect, or hope, it remains a source of motivation.<br>
At 10th level, the attacks triggered by your At my command feature benefit from your charisma modifier to the attack roll.<br>
Moreover you gain two more uses of your Leader feature.

## Officer is always right
At 15th level, the attacks triggered by your At my command feature benefit from your charisma modifier to the damage roll.<br>
Moreover, you gain advantage on Persuasion checks about strategy.

## Coat of medals
All your victories are pinned to your clothes and you should think about wearing a bigger hat to create more space.<br>
At 18th level, you gain expertise with Persuasion and Intimidation.<br>
Moreover you gain two more uses of your Leader feature.
