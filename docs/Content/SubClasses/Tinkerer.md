# Technician: Tinkerer Specialist

Your equipment uses your Intelligence modifier.

## Armorer
You gain proficiency with all armors and shields.

## Jack of all Forges
Beginning when you choose this specialty at 3rd level, you learn multiple Modifications you can apply to weapons and armor.<br>
During a long rest, using Tinkerer's tools, you can apply a number of Modifications equal to your proficiency bonus.<br>
When trying to apply a Modification, make a Tinker's tools roll against a DC of 12 + 4 for each Modification already present on the object. On a failure, you lose one attempt.

Modification         | Type         | Effect
---------------------|--------------|-------
Joint Clearance      | Armor        | Remove the disadvantage on Stealth of an armor but reduce the AC by one.
Bulky                | Armor        | Increase the AC by one, but reduce dexterity by two.
Additional pockets   | Armor        | Increase the number of Gadget Points by your Intelligence modifier.
Thermal Goggles      | Armor        | Attach these goggles to the helmet. The user don't need proficiency to use them. Excludes other goggles.
Night-vision Goggles | Armor        | Attach these goggles to the helmet. The user don't need proficiency to use them. Excludes other goggles.
Legs Exoskeleton     | Armor        | Increase the walking speed by 5ft.
Arms Exoskeleton     | Armor        | Add melee damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
Zapzap               | Weapon       | Add the Shocking property to a weapon.
Ionic                | Weapon       | Add <span style="color:mediumslateblue">ionic</span> damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
Igniter              | Weapon       | Add <span style="color:darkorange">fire</span> damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
Zooming Scope        | Range weapon | You don't have disadvantage on your shoots outside the base range.
Silencer             | Blaster      | Reduce the emitted sound when shooting to 15 feet. The laser shots also become less bright.
Battery              | Blaster      | Increase the blaster max overheat by 50%.
Nitrogen Condensator | Blaster      | Increase the active cooling by 1 (no effect if original cooling is 1).

You can apply one Modification by weapon or armor. You can apply two at 9th level and three at 18th level.<br>
You can't apply the same Modification multiple times on the same object.

## That's just a moving armor
At 6th level, you can use your armorer skills to improve your droid companion defenses.<br>
Your droid companion gain AC equal to your Intelligence modifier.

## Flash of Genius
At 9th level, you've gained the ability to come up with solutions under pressure. When you or another creature you can see within 30 feet of you makes an ability check or a saving throw, you can use your reaction to add your Intelligence modifier to the roll.
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## The Right Tool for the Job
At 14th level, you've learned how to produce exactly the tool you need.<br>
With Tinkerer's tools in hand and enough scrap around you, you can create a gadget or tool.<br>
This creation requires 1 hour of uninterrupted work, which can coincide with a short or long rest.<br>
The resulting object remains a makeshift creation, thus it is destroyed after on use, but this use is free (no Gadget Points).

## Master gunsmith
At 18th level, your Modifications are improved as following.

Modification         | Type         | Improvement
---------------------|--------------|------------
Joint Clearance      | Armor        | No more AC reduction.
Bulky                | Armor        | No more Dexterity reduction
Additional pockets   | Armor        | Increase again the number of Gadget Points by your Intelligence modifier.
Thermal Goggles      | Armor        | The user gets +2 on attacks against an enemy blinded by smoke when using these goggles. 
Night-vision Goggles | Armor        | The user gets +2 on attacks against an enemy blinded by darkness when using these goggles.
Legs Exoskeleton     | Armor        | Now Increase the walking speed by 10ft.
Arms Exoskeleton     | Armor        | Add 1d4 to melee damage.
Zapzap               | Weapon       | Add 1d4 <span style="color:dodgerblue">lightning</span> damage.
Ionic                | Weapon       | Add 1d4 <span style="color:mediumslateblue">ionic</span> damage.
Igniter              | Weapon       | Add 1d4 <span style="color:darkorange">fire</span> damage.
Zooming Scope        | Range weapon | Add 1d4 to the attack rolls.
Silencer             | Blaster      | Now reduce the emitted sound when shooting to 10 feet. The laser shots become almost transparent.
Battery              | Blaster      | Now Increase the blaster max overheat by 100%.
Nitrogen Condensator | Blaster      | Now Increase the active cooling by 2 (or 1 if original cooling is 1).
