# Soldier: Purifier

Your equipment uses your Intelligence modifier.

## Pyromaniac
You are not a pyromaniac, you just love fire and the fires that break out after you pass by are just a coincidence.\
Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with flamethrowers and hand flamethrowers.\
Moreover, the <span style="color:darkorange">fire</span> damage you deal is increased by your Charisma modifier, even to yourself (but you like it).\
If you use a jetpack, you automatically know the Scorching Landing maneuver. Those kinds of things are natural to you.

## The smell of napalm in the morning
At 7th level, your flamethrowers and incendiary grenade use a special liquid, preventing the target to stop [Burning](../Conditions.md) by using an action.\
Moreover any [Burning](../Conditions.md) you inflict last for one more turn.

## Fire of joy
At 10th level, seeing people burn makes you happy, and also terrifying. For each person with the [Burning](../Conditions.md) within 30 feet of you, you gain a +1 temporary bonus to your Charisma.

## Wookie knightmare
At 15th level, you improve your secret recipe, and any [Burning](../Conditions.md) you inflict last fo another additional turn.\
Additionally, even if a target succeeds against your attempt to ignite them, they still get one turn (and no more) of [Burning](../Conditions.md).

## Some people want to watch the world burn...
...and you are one of them.\
At 18th level, the <span style="color:darkorange">fire</span> damage you deal is now increased by your Charisma modifier * 2, and your flamethrowers and incendiary grenades gain 5 feet of range.
