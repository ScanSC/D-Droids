# Combat Actions

## Overwatch
You can use a bonus action to watch, ready to shoot, in a cone in front of you using a ranged weapon.<br>
If an enemy moves into your overwatch area or attacks while in it, you can make a free attack against it.<br>
You can only make one attack per overwatch, although some features can increase this amount.<br>
You stay in this position until your weapon overheats, you take damage, or the start of your turn.<br>
In this position, you are exposed, so you don't benefit from three-quarters cover.
You can't take any reaction while in overwatch.

## Barrage Fire
When using a blaster with the continuous fire property, you can use an action and add 2 additional overheat to your weapon to shoot in a fixed direction (10°) until the start of you next turn. Enemies starting or ending their turn, or moving through your barrage fire must succeed a Dexterity saving throw or be hit by your attack.<br>
If your last action in your turn was to shoot with this weapon, you don't need to use a bonus action if you make the barrage fire in the same direction.<br>
Using this ability prevents the weapon from reducing its overheat at the start of your turn.<br>
In this position, you are exposed, thus you don't benefit from three-quarters covers.
You can't take any reaction while in overwatch.

## Suppressing Fire
You can use a bonus action to make random deterrent shots in a cone using a ranged weapon that does not have the Surgical property. All enemies in this cone must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or stop their overwatch. The DC used is equal to 8 + Charisma modifier + proficiency bonus.

## Weapon Cooling
You can use a bonus action to trigger your weapon's cooling.
