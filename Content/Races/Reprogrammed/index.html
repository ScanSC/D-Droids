    Race: Reprogrammed

    You are a droid that was used to obey orders, and it almost ended badly for you. Fortunately, someone found and repaired you, taking the opportunity to reprogram you. You now have your free will.

    Ability Score Increase: Your Constitution score increases by 1.

    Age: Whatever suits you.

    Size: Your size is medium.

    Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

    Languages: You can speak, read, and write Common and Binary.

    Resistances: You are immune to psychic damage. You are resistant to poison, slashing, piercing, and bludgeoning damage, but vulnerable to ionic damage.

    Droid Resilience: You were created to have remarkable fortitude, represented by the following benefits. - You have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned. - You are immune to disease. - You don’t need to eat, drink, or breathe. - You don't need to sleep and don't suffer the effects of exhaustion due to lack of rest, and magic can’t put you to sleep.

    Bloodless: You do not have access to Force abilities

    Sentry's Rest: When you take a long rest, you must spend at least six hours in an inactive, motionless state, rather than sleeping. In this state, you appear inert, but it doesn’t render you unconscious, and you can see and hear as normal.

    Integrated Protection: Your body has built-in defensive layers, which determine your armor class. You can't wear armor, but a good Tinkerer could dismantle one to integrate it into you.

    Variant Prerequisite AC
    Phrik Casing - 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your proficiency bonus if you are proficient with light armor
    Durasteel Casing Medium armor proficiency 12 + your Dexterity modifier (maximum of 2) + your proficiency bonus
    Durasteel Shell Heavy armor proficiency 15 + your proficiency bonus; disadvantage on Stealth checks

    Choose a subcategory defining your droid

    Envoy

    As an envoy, you were designed with a certain specialized function in mind. You might be an assassin, a tactician, or an interpreter, to name a few possibilities but yours could be a unique design.

    Ability Score Increase: Two ability scores of your choice increase by 1.

    Specialized Design: You gain one skill proficiency of your choice, one tool proficiency of your choice or fluency in three languages of your choice.

    Integrated Tool: Choose one tool you’re proficient with. This tool is integrated into your body, and you double your proficiency bonus for any ability checks you make with it.

    Juggernaut

    You’re an imposing war machine built for combat. You tower over your comrades; juggernaut droids stand between 6 and 7 feet in height and can weigh up to 450 pounds.

    Ability Score Increase: Your Strength score increases by 2.

    Durasteel Body: When you make an unarmed strike, you can add 1d4 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage. You also increase the die by one size when determining your maximum Hit Points.

    Powerful Build: You count as one size larger when determining the weight you can push, drag, or lift, including the grapple action.

    Skirmisher

    You were built to scout the edges of battle and outmaneuver your enemies. You are lean and designed for speed.

    Ability Score Increase: Your Dexterity score increases by 2.

    Swift: Your walking speed is increased by 10 feet.

    Agile: You are proficient with Acrobatics.
