    Class Features

    As a Force Sensitive, you gain the following class features.

    Hit Points

    Hit Dice: 1d8 per Force Sensitive level
    Hit Points at 1st Level: 8 + your Constitution modifier
    Hit Points at Higher Levels: 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per Force Sensitive level after 1st

    Proficiencies

    Armor: light common armors, jedi armors
    Weapons: Common weapons
    Equipment: -
    Saving Throws: Constitution, Wisdom
    Skills: Choose two skills from Acrobatics, Insight, History, Arcane, Animal Handling, and Survival

    Equipment

    You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background: - a light common armor or a common weapon - a common weapon

    Equipment Choices

    The Force Sensitive table shows how many equipments you can carry, and how many points you have to use gadgets.
    You regain all expended gadget points when you finish a long rest.
    At 1st level and every level that increases the number of equipment choices, choose one piece of equipment from the list.

    Force Power

    The Force within you offers you supernatural abilities.
    - 5ft Blindsight - Your jumps are empowered by 10ft in length and height - Your falling damage is reduced by your Wisdom modifier * proficiency bonus - Using a bonus action, you can move small objects such as some weapons, a mouse, a glass etc.. All your Force abilities have a 30ft range.

    Force Domain

    At 1st level, you choose a domain shaped by your choices and your ethic.

    Domain | ------------------------------------------------| Domain of the Wizard Domain of the Knight Domain of Dathomir

    Force Tricks

    At 2nd level, you learn other ways to use your powers.
    You have multiple Force Dies, evolving with you, as shown is the Force Sensitive table.
    Each Force Trick consume one Force Die when used, and you can consume more to increase their effect (number in bold) by the same amount of dice. However, you can spend a maximum number of Force Dice equal to your proficiency bonus on one Force Trick.
    A trick need an action and can be used as an extra attack and has a 30ft range.
    Choose three tricks among the list below.

    Trick Casting time Effect
    Sabotage Bonus action You make a blaster shoot. If a creature is in front of the blaster, it must succeed a Dexterity saving throw or being hit by the shot.
    Boomerang Action You throw a melee weapon with the light property to a target. Make a Wisdom attack, dealing the weapon damage + 1 Force Die damage of the same type. The weapon come back in your hand.
    Disarm Action You try to make a weapon slip from its wielder's hands. The target makes a Strength saving throw or sees its weapon thrown 10 feet in a direction of your choice.
    Rising Bonus action You try to make a target levitate up to 10ft until the start of your next turn. The target must succeed a Constitution saving throw, but can fail it on purpose. A levitating target can't move unless it can grab something (Strength saving throw to get free) or use a jetpack (get free).
    Repulsion Action Your target is struck by an invisible force dealing 1 Force Die force damage. The target makes a Strength saving throw: on a failure, it is pushed 10 feet away from you.
    Attraction Action Your target is grabbed by an invisible force dealing 1 Force Die force damage. The target makes a Strength saving throw: on a failure, it is pushed 10 feet toward you.
    Hurl Action You can hurl a small object at your target. Make a Wisdom attack, dealing the object damage + 1 Force Die damage of the same type. The 30ft range is for the object, and it can be thrown at 30ft.
    Catch-up Reaction When you see something or someone fall, you reduce the fall damage by your wisdom modifier + 1 Force Die. If the total amount is greater than the distance of the fall in feet, the target is suspended in the air and you can make it levitate toward you.
    Suggestion Action You can try to manipulate a creature. It must succeed a Wisdom saving throw or you can give it one simple instruction. The creature don't realise by itself what happened. The creature must not be in a fight except it's a beast.
    Detection Bonus action You can detect any organic creature around you. All creatures roll a Stealth check against 8 + 1 Force Die to stay hidden from you.
    Amplification - When you succeed a unarmed attack roll, you can add 1 Force Die damage.
    Healing Trance 1 minute You can enter in a deep trance to heal your wounds. You regain 1 Force Die + your Wisdom modifier hit points per consumed dice.

    The save DC of your tricks is equal to 8 + consumed Force Dice. You choose an other trick at 4th, 6th, 11th, 14th and 18th levels.

    Unarmored Defense

    Beginning at 3rd level, while you are wearing no armor and not wielding a shield, your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Wisdom modifier.

    Ability Score Improvement

    When you reach 4th level, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

    Force Versatility (Optional)

    Whenever you reach a level in this class that grants the Ability Score Improvement feature, you can replace a force trick you know with another one.
    If you don't replace a force trick, you can replace one gadget by another one.

    Voice of Nature

    At 7th level, you gain expertise with Nature or Animal Handling.

    Stillness of mind

    At 10th level, you gain the ability to diffuse violent situations.
    Whenever you make a Charisma check to calm violent emotions or to counsel peace, you have advantage on the roll.
    You must make this entreaty in good faith; it doesn’t apply if proficiency in the Deception or Intimidation skill applies to your check.

    You also gain proficiency in the Performance or Persuasion skill (choose one).

    Wind steps

    At 13th level, you can use Dash with a bonus action.
    Moreover your walking speed increase by 10ft.

    Evasion

    At 17th level, your instinctive agility lets you dodge out of the way of certain area effects.
    When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw, and only half damage if you fail.

    Midichlorian Rush

    At 20th level, when you roll for initiative and have less than 6 Force Dies remaining, you regain Force Dies up to a total of 6.

    One With the Force

    At 20th level, you can enter in an etheral form, but there is no going back.
