    Class Features

    As a bounty hunter, you gain the following class features.

    Hit Points

    Hit Dice: 1d8 per Bounty Hunter level
    Hit Points at 1st Level: 8 + your Constitution modifier
    Hit Points at Higher Levels: 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per Bounty Hunter level after 1st

    Proficiencies

    Armor: light and medium common armors, all common shields, small ionic shields
    Weapons: Common and Military weapons
    Equipment: All grenades, Laser Cutter
    Saving Throws: Strength, Wisdom
    Skills: Choose two skills from Acrobatics, Athletics, Insight, Deception, Intimidation, Investigation, Sleight of Hand and Survival

    Equipment

    You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background: - a light or medium common armor - two common or military weapons or one common or military weapon and a shield - a tool you are proficient with

    Equipment Choices

    The bounty hunter table shows how much equipment you can carry and how many points you have to use on gadgets.
    You regain all expended gadget points when you finish a long rest.
    At 1st level and every level that increases the number of equipment choices, choose one piece of equipment from the list.

    Fighting Style

    You adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. Choose one of the following options. You can't take a Fighting Style option more than once, even if you later get to choose again. - Duelist: At the start of your first turn, you can make one free attack with a light weapon. You have advantage on this attack. - Fencer: When you are wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon. - Sprinkler: When shooting with a blaster and the blaster does not have the continuous fire property, you can add 1 additional overheat to your weapon to get +4 to the attack roll. - Blind fighting: You have blindsight with a range of 10 feet. Within that range, you can effectively see anything that isn't behind total cover, even if you're blinded or in darkness. Moreover, you can see an invisible creature within that range, unless the creature successfully hides from you. - Unarmed Fighting: Your unarmed strikes can deal bludgeoning damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier on a hit. If you aren't wielding any weapons or a shield when you make the attack roll, the d6 becomes a d8. At the start of each of your turns, you can deal 1d4 bludgeoning damage to one creature grappled by you. - Cool-headed: You have advantage on saving throws against suppressing fire actions. Moreover when making a ranged attack while you are within 5 feet of a hostile creature, you do not have disadvantage on the attack roll. - Revenge: When a creature you can see misses an attack against you, you can use your reaction to make an attack against it while the weapon used does not have the Charging property. - Farsighted: You gain one more equipment choice and two more gadget points. - Akimbo: When using one light blaster in both hands, you can fire with both as part of the same action, but with a penalty of -5 on the two attack rolls.

    Martial Arts

    At 1st level, your professional activities often lead you to hand-to-hand combat.

    You gain the following benefits while you are unarmed or wielding only light melee weapons, and you aren't wearing a heavy armor or wielding a shield:

    • You can use Dexterity instead of Strength for the attack and damage rolls of your unarmed strikes and melee weapons.
    • You can roll a d4 and add it to the normal damage of your unarmed strike or melee weapon.
    • When you use the Attack action with an unarmed strike or a melee weapon on your turn, you can make one unarmed strike as a bonus action. For example, if you take the Attack action and attack with a quarterstaff, you can also make an unarmed strike as a bonus action, assuming you haven't already taken a bonus action this turn.
    • When an enemy attacks you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to try to parry the attack. You must choose to take this reaction before knowing whether the attack hits. Make a Dexterity check against its attack roll. On a success, the attack misses you and the enemy can't attack you again until the end of its turn. On a failure, the attack hits.

    Data collector

    At 2nd level, you learn to collect information from people around you.
    Whenever you question someone to learn about someone, something, or somewhere, you add your Wisdom modifier to your Insight, Persuasion, Deception, or Intimidation checks.

    Hunting Tradition

    At 3rd level, you commit yourself to a hunting tradition. Your tradition grants you features at 3rd level and again at 6th, 9th, 11th, and 17th level.

    Tradition | ---------------------------------------------------| Way of the Assassin Way of the Poisoner Way of the Specialist Way of the Trapper

    Ability Score Improvement

    When you reach 4th level, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

    Hunter Versatility (Optional)

    Whenever you reach a level in this class that grants the Ability Score Improvement feature, you can replace a fighting style you know with another fighting style available to Bounty Hunters.
    If you don't replace a fighting style, you can replace one gadget with another.

    Extra Attack

    Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

    The number of attacks increases to three when you reach 15th level in this class.

    Professional Tracker

    After finding your target, losing it is the worst thing that could happen.
    At 7th level, your trackers are no more limited to your solar system.
    Moreover, you have advantage on Survival checks to track something.

    Reputation

    You have completed many contracts and are known in the underworld.
    At 13th level, you add your proficiency bonus to your Intimidation checks.
    You always find an open door among all the underworld bigwigs, unless there is a bounty on their heads. Additionally, any contract reward in credits is increased by 10% per Charisma modifier.

    Reliable Talent

    By 14th level, you have refined your chosen skills until they approach perfection. Whenever you make an ability check that lets you add your proficiency bonus, you can treat a d20 roll of 5 or lower as a 6.

    Superior Fighter

    At 18th level, your bonus damage from your Martial Arts feature is now a d8 instead of a d4. Moreover, if you successfully parry an enemy attack, you can make one free unarmed strike or attack with a light weapon against this target.

    Facing Death

    At 20th level, if you lock eyes with a creature you want to see dead, it has to make a Wisdom saving throw against your Intimidation check. On a failure, the creature is terrified of you until it learns that you died or that you no longer want to kill it. If the margin of failure exceeds 10, the target faints.
