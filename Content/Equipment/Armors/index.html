    Properties

    Warm

    You are resistant to cold damage from the environment.

    Flexible

    Your armor is flexible, granting you resistance to bludgeoning damage.

    Unbreakable x

    Damage that you take is reduced by x, except for force and psychic damage.

    Force protection

    You have advantage on saving throws against Force powers.

    Cold fibers

    You are invisible to thermal devices.

    Beskar

    You are resistant to radiant damage.

    Disruptive

    You are resistant to damage from lightsabers, and a lightsaber that hits you becomes disrupted and can't attack until the start of its wielder's next turn.

    Fragile

    The composition of your armor is weak to explosions. If you get hit by an explosion, roll a d20: on a 3 or less, the armor breaks and you no longer benefit from it.

    Complete cover

    Your armor protects you from various dangers. While wearing the full armor, you gain resistance to poison and acid damage.

    Resistant

    Your armor protects you from blades and spikes. While wearing the full armor, you gain resistance to slashing and piercing damage.

    Hands free

    This shield is attached to your arm, keeping your hand free.

    Ionic shield x - 1dy

    A ionic shield take more easily the shots but have a limited capacity.
    x represent the maximal Hit Points of the shield. Every time you get hit by an attack roll, the shield takes the damages. If the damages exceed the shield remaining Hit Points, you still get hit but the damages are reduced by the Hit Points the shield had.
    At the beginning of your turn, the shield gain 1dy Hit Points.
    Ionic shields are luminous, thus giving you disadvantage on Stealth checks, but you can turn them off.

    Pressurized

    The armor protects you from gas and the vacuum of space. You can breathe in the vacuum of space for 10 minutes, recharging when entering a breathable area. You are immune to poison.
