    Blasters and Range

    Ranged weapons use your Dexterity for attack and damage rolls.

    Overheat and Cooling

    When you shoot with a blaster, you increase the overheat of your weapon by 1.
    If a shot exceeds the maximum overheat the blaster can manage (shown in the overheat column), you take fire damage equal to the blaster's damage roll plus the amount by which the limit was exceeded. Moreover, you also roll a d20: on a 1, the weapon breaks and gains the Broken property; on a 20, the weapon returns to its maximum overheat value and you don't take overheat damage.
    At the start of your turn, your weapon's overheat is reduced by 1. Using the Weapon Cooling action, you roll one cooling die and reduce the overheat by the amount rolled.

    Properties

    Bright

    Your weapon emits light, giving you disadvantage on Stealth checks. You can turn it off.

    Parry

    Your weapon allows you to parry weapons that deal radiant damage.
    When an attack hits you, you can use your reaction to try to parry it.
    Make an attack roll with your weapon against your attacker's roll. On a success, you don't take any damage. Rolling a 20 with a lightsaber returns the attack to the attacker.

    Double bladed

    After you take the Attack action against a target, you can make another attack dealing 1d4 damage against that target using your bonus action.

    Rotative

    You can make your weapon quickly rotate. Your parry attack roll have a +2 bonus.
    Moreover you can use both your action and bonus action to make an attack on every creature around you in your reach. Make an attack roll for each.

    Hidden

    Your weapon is hidden or doesn't look like a weapon.
    People will not consider you to be armed because of these weapons.

    Grappling

    You can use this weapon to grapple a target.
    You can grapple using the weapon's reach and use an Acrobatics check instead of Athletics. The weapon can be used as a Grappling Hook gadget but with its own range.

    Beskar Blade

    Your weapon is made of beskar, thus its damage ignore resistance to its damage.

    Light

    A light weapon is small and easy to handle, making it ideal for use when fighting with two weapons. A blaster with the light property can be fired using a bonus action.

    Heavy

    Small creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls with heavy weapons. A heavy weapon’s size and bulk make it too large for a Small creature to use effectively.

    Two-handed

    This weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

    Burst

    The blaster can shoot bursts.
    A burst shoot increase the damage die by one size (d12 max) and damage by 1.
    You can apply this effect multiple times one the same attack.
    For each die size above the original one, the blaster gain one more overheat with the attack.

    Charging

    The weapon need time before firing. You can't make overwatch attack with this weapon.

    Area

    The weapon attack affect a whole area, defined by its range and overwatch angle. You don't have to make attack roll, but your enemy have to succeed a dexterity saving throw to take half the damages

    Continuous fire

    You gain the barrage fire action.

    Shocking

    You can activate a shocking mode modifying the effect of your attacks: - the weapon's damage is converted to lightning damage - a creature hit can’t take reactions until the start of its next turn - the range is reduced to 10ft if it's a range weapon

    Polyvalent

    This weapon is made to be used with two hands, but you can use it with one hand with a -3 penalty to attack rolls.

    Surgical

    This weapon is designed for taking few, but precise, shots. Thus you can't take the suppressing fire action with it.

    Broken

    If your weapon is broken, you can't take the Weapon Cooling bonus action anymore. Moreover each time you shoot with it, roll a d20.
