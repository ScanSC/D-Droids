    Equipment

    Common Gadgets

    Gadgets consume gadget points (gp) defined by your class.
    Common gadgets can be used by anyone and don't need any proficiency

    Stims are small injection devices. During your turn, you can throw a stim to an ally up to 20 feet away. Make a DC 10 Athletics check. On a success, the ally can use their reaction to make a DC 8 Dexterity check to catch and use the stim. On a failure, the ally must wait until their turn and use a bonus action to pick up and use the stim.

    Healing Stim

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Target: A creature you touch
    Cost: 2 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    You press the stim against the skin, and a liquid is injected. The creature regains a number of hit points equal to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier.
    It does nothing on droids.

    Regenerating Stim

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Target: A creature you touch
    Cost: 2 gp
    Duration: 3 turns
    You press the stim against the skin, and a liquid is injected.
    At use and at the start of its turn until the effect ends, the creature regains a number of hit points equal to 1d4.
    It does nothing on droids.

    Pain Inhibitor stim

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Target: A creature you touch
    Cost: 2 gp
    Duration: 1 minute
    You press the stim against the skin, and a liquid is injected.
    The creature gains the Pain Inhibited condition for the duration.
    It does nothing on droids.

    Haste stim

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Target: A creature you touch
    Cost: 3 gp
    Duration: 3 turns
    You press the stim against the skin, and a liquid is injected.
    Until the effect ends, the target gains an additional action on each of its turns.
    When the effect ends, the target can’t move or take actions until after its next turn, as a wave of lethargy sweeps over it.
    It does nothing on droids.

    Adrenaline stim

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Target: A creature you touch
    Cost: 3 gp
    Duration: 3 turns
    You press the stim against the skin, and a liquid is injected.
    Until the effect ends, the target gains an additional bonus action on each of its turns.
    When the effect ends, the target can’t move or take actions until after its next turn, as a wave of lethargy sweeps over it.
    It does nothing on droids.

    Rush stim

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Target: A creature you touch
    Cost: 3 gp
    Duration: 3 turns
    You press the stim against the skin, and a liquid is injected.
    Until the effect ends, the target gains an additional bonus action on each of its turns.
    When the effect ends, the target can’t move or take actions until after its next turn, as a wave of lethargy sweeps over it.
    It does nothing on droids.

    Bait

    Using Time: 1 action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 4 gp
    Duration: 5 turns
    A realistic hologram of yourself is created. The hologram plays random animations such as bending down, raising its weapon, or pulling out a gadget.
    A creature hostile to you considers it a real enemy, but after attacking the hologram, it makes an Insight check against your gadget DC to understand that it's bait.

    Taser

    Using Time: 1 action
    Range: Touch
    Cost: 5 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    You release an electric shock on the target, dealing 1d8 lightning non-lethal damage.
    The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or is paralyzed until the end of its next turn.

    Sepcial Gadgets

    Medikit

    Using Time: 1 action and 1 bonus action
    Target: A creature you touch
    Cost: 5 gp
    Duration: 3 turns
    A medical kit with everything you need.
    You make a Medicine ability check.
    The creature regains a number of hit points according to the table below.
    Result | Amount -------|------- 1(nat) | 1d8 necrotic damage 2-5 | 1d8 + wisdom modifier 6-10 | 2d8 + wisdom modifier 11-15 | 3d8 + wisdom modifier 16-19 | 4d8 + wisdom modifier 20(nat)| 32 + wisdom modifier It does nothing on droids and you can't use it on yourself.

    Rocket Launcher

    Using Time: 1 action
    Range: 150 feet
    Cost: 5 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    A bright streak flashes from your rocket launcher to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into an explosion of flame.
    Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw against your gadget DC. A target takes 4d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

    Flamethrower

    Using Time: 1 action
    Range: Touch
    Cost: 3 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    Each creature in a 20-foot and 35° cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 2d4 fire damage on a failed save and one turn of Burning, or half as much damage on a successful one without Burning.

    Hand Flamethrower

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: Touch
    Cost: 2 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    Each creature in a 10-foot and 45° cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 1d6 fire damage on a failed save and one turn of Burning, or half as much damage on a successful one without Burning.

    Thermal detonator

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 3 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    You throw the grenade on a point in range.
    Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 2d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

    Sticky thermal detonator

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 4 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    You make an attack roll using your Dexterity to throw the grenade at a point in range. On a failure, the grenade acts like a normal thermal detonator. On a hit, the target automatically fails its saving throw.
    Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 2d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
    This grenade can't roll.

    EMP Grenade

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 3 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    You throw the grenade on a point in range. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 2d6 ionic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

    Incendiary Grenade

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 3 gp
    Duration: 1 minute
    You throw the grenade on a point in range. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 1d8 fire damage on a failed save and one turn of Burning, or half as much damage on a successful one without Burning.

    Smoke Grenade

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 3 gp
    Duration: 3 turns
    You throw the grenade on a point in range. The grenade creates a 10-foot-radius sphere of opaque smoke.
    Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point becomes Blinded until it gets out of the smoke or the smoke effect ends.
    Attacks from outside the smoke have disadvantage.

    Flash Grenade

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 2 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    You throw the grenade on a point in range. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Constitution saving throw. A target becomes Blinded until the end of its next turn on a failed save.

    Gas Grenade

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 3 gp
    Duration: 3 turns
    You throw the grenade on a point in range. The grenade creates a 10-foot-radius sphere of poisonous, yellow-green fog.
    Each creature inside must make a Constitution saving throw. A target takes 1d6 poison damage on a failed save and become poisoned until the end of his next turn, or half as much damage on a successful one without being poisoned.

    Paralyzing Grenade

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 4 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    You throw the grenade on a point in range. Each creature in a 5-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Constitution saving throw. A target takes 1d6 lightning damage on a failed save and become stunned until the end of his next turn, or half as much damage on a successful one without being stunned.

    Deployable barrier

    Using Time: 1 action
    Range: Touch
    Cost: 2 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    You deploy the barrier in front of you. The barrier is 10 feet wide and 2 feet high, offering a half cover.
    The barrier is an ionic shield with Hit Points equal to a number of d8 equal to your gadget ability modifier and works as an ionic shield.

    Ionic Dome

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet + 5 ft * Strength modifier
    Cost: 4 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    The dome is deployed at impact. The dome covers a 5-foot-radius sphere and offers total cover. The dome is an ionic shield with Hit Points equal to a number of d12 equal to your gadget ability modifier and works as an ionic shield.

    Grappling Hook

    Using Time: 1 bonus action
    Range: 20 feet
    Cost: 1 gp
    Duration: Instantaneous
    Using 1 Gadget Point, make a ranged attack roll against a creature within 20 feet to hook it: - If the creature is the same size as you, you can move it 10 feet closer to yourself in a straight line. The creature must succeed on a Strength saving throw or fall prone. - If the creature is larger than you, you can retract the rope to try to ride it. If you shoot at a surface, you don't need an attack roll, and you can retract the rope to reach that position.

    Common Tools

    Common tools can be used by anyone and don't need any proficiency

    Binoculars

    Allow you to see and listen from far away.
    Using a bonus action, you make a DC 10 + 2 per 30 feet from the chosen target Perception check. On a success you double your proficiency bonus on your next attack roll on this target for this turn.

    Thermal Goggles

    Make you able to see warm creatures and objects through smokes up to 60 feet.

    Night-vision Goggles

    Make you able to see in the dark up to 60 feet.

    Tracker

    A discreet tracking device. It can be magnetized.
    By looking at your remote or another device linked to the tracker, you can know the distance and direction from the tracker.
    The tracker works only if you are in the same solar system.

    Flashlight

    A simple flashlight illuminating in a 45° cone bright light in a 20-foot range and dim light for an additional 20 feet.
    Can be attached to a blaster.

    Special Tools

    Stim Pistol

    Using Time: 1 action
    Range: 30 feet
    Cost: the stim cost
    The stim pistol allows you to shoot stim directly to a creature.
    You have to make an attack roll against the creature, but the target can be willing to be touched, thus removing his Dexterity modifier from his AC.
    On a fail the stim bounce and fall on the ground.

    Defibrillator

    Using Time: 1 action
    Target: A creature you touch
    Duration: Instantaneous
    A shocking helping hand.
    If the target is conscious, you make a melee attack roll against it using Dexterity. The attack hit for 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier lightning damage. If the creature is unconscious, it come back to 1 hit point.

    Jetpack

    Using Time: 1 bonus action - 1 / short rest
    You can use your jetpack to quickly fly to locations, cross a chasm, reach a height, or levitate above dangerous ground.
    Interacting with the jetpack, you can activate the levitation, lifting you a few tens of centimeters off the ground and protecting you from dangerous ground. The levitation give you a speed of 20ft in the spatial void.
    Using a bonus action, you gain 30ft of flying speed.

    If you end your turn in the air, you slowly descends back to the ground with the levitation active.

    Bomb

    Using Time: 1 minute / 3 turns with proficiency
    You need to make something big look like a big pile of gravel? You have found what you are looking for.
    During the deployment, you can either choose to set a timer or to bind it to a detonator.
    When exploding, all creatures in a 50ft radius sphere must succeed a Dexterity saving throw or take 10d100 fire damage on a fail, and the same amount on a success.

    Tinker's tool

    A set of tinker's tools is designed to enable you to repair many objects. You won't be fixing the Death Star with it, but it always comes in handy.

    Hacking tool

    A type of screwdriver capable of connecting to most systems. It can allow you to open doors, shut down cameras, or, with a lot of skill, reprogram a droid.

    Laser Cutter

    This cutter looks like a big pen and can cut through most metals.
