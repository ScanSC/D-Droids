    Technician: Droid Specialist

    Your equipment uses your Intelligence modifier.

    Improved Droid

    Beginning when you choose this specialty at 3rd level, you spend time working on your droid companion.
    You can now give upgrades to your droid. The droid can have an amount of upgrades equal to your proficiency modifier.
    You can replace an upgrade by another one once during a long rest where you don't repair your droid.

    Upgrade Effect
    Reinforced Plate Give +1 to the droid AC. This upgrade can be applied multiple times.
    Smaller Vital Components Increase the droid constitution by 2. This upgrade can be applied multiple times.
    Repairability Index: 10 When repairing your droid, roll a d8 instead of a d6.
    Thrusters Give the droid a 20ft fly speed.
    Propulsion Increase the droid walk speed by 10ft.
    Exterminate Increase the Zapzap instruction damage by 1d6.
    Advanced Scanner When using the Scanning instruction, the affected creatures lose 1 AC until the end of your turn.
    Farsighted Droid Add two additional uses of emergency stim. This upgrade can be applied multiple times.
    Opposable Thumbs The droid can hold and use a blaster with the Light property. It uses Intelligence for attack rolls. It replaces one of your attacks.
    Grenade Launcher The droid can throw grenades you are equipped with. The throwing range increases by 10 ft. It uses your gadget points. It replaces one of your attacks.
    Copilot The droid can connect to a vehicle interface to add your intelligence modifier to pilot rolls.

    Emergency Protocol

    At 6th level, you setup an emergency protocol for your droid. The emergency protocol trigger when the droid Hit Points drop to 0.
    Once the emergency protocol triggered, it don't happen again until you've repaired the droid during a rest.

    Choose one option among those three : - Last stand: The droid recover 1 hit points - Mutual destruction: The droid explodes, except if you are in range. Every creature within 10 feet must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + proficiency bonus + Constitution modifier). On a failure, the creature takes a number of d10 fire damage equal to your proficiency modifier, or half the damage on a success. Set the droid's Hit Points to -12. - Protection: The droid emits an ionic dome with a radius of 10 feet and with Hit Points equal to your Intelligence modifier * your proficiency bonus.

    Synchronization

    At 9th level, whenever you attack a creature, your droid can attack (Zapzap, Opposable Thumbs, Grenade Launcher) the same target for free.
    You can use this feature once each turn.

    My Friend

    At 14th level, if your droid falls to 0 Hit Points, you have advantage on attack rolls against the creature that deals the damage until the end of combat.
    If you fall to 0 HitPoints, you can give on last instruction to your droid, it can be to flee, or to attack the creature that deals the damage. The droid has advantage on attack rolls until the end of the combat.

    What would I do without it?

    At 18th level, whenever your droid is within 10ft from you, you get a bonus equal to your Intelligence modifier to any ability check or saving throw.
