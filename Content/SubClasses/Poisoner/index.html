    Bounty Hunter: Poisoner

    Your equipment uses your Intelligence modifier.

    Poisoner kit

    Beginning when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with stim pistols.
    Your healing stim and regenerating stim deal poison or acid damage (choose when you use it) instead of healing.
    Moreover, your gas grenades can deal acid damage instead of poison damage and use your Intelligence modifier.

    Reversed metabolism

    At 3rd level, your countless experiments on yourself with poisons and chemicals have modified your metabolism. Healing effects deal damage to you, but poison damage heals you. You are also resistant to acid and immune to poisoning.

    Side Effects

    At 6th level, when a creature is affected by one of your poisoned stims, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become poisoned until the end of its next turn.

    Rabbit Hole

    At 9th level, you’ve read and watched everything there is to see about poisons and venomous creatures.
    You know if a creature is venomous and you have advantage on Animal Handling checks with them.
    Moreover, your poison and acid damage ignore resistances.

    Arsenic pudding

    The thing you love about poisons is that when you mix together just about any dangerous substances, the effect is always brutal.
    At 11th level, all your poison and acid damage is increased by your Intelligence modifier.

    Ultimate Poison

    At 17th level, your poison damage ignores immunity, and any poisoning effect you inflict lasts for one more turn.
