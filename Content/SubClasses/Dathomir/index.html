    Force Sensitive: Dathomir

    Everything you learned comes from Allya, the mother of Dathomir.

    Your character story must align with the practices of the Dathomiri society.

    Your equipment uses your Charisma modifier.

    Dathomiri Practices

    The practitioners of Dathomir Magic believed themselves to be casting spells rather than making use of the Force, and often were unable to make use of a "spell" without speaking its assigned name or performing the ritualized singing, dancing, chanting, and/or writing associated with it. The Nightsisters often attributed the work of the Force to the Spirits instead.

    You learned to use the Force from the Dathomir witches. Choose one clan of Dathomir: if you choose the Nightsisters, use the alternative features.
    Whenever the Force Sensitive class mentions Wisdom, replace it with Charisma.
    You have advantage on any skill rolls involving rancors.

    Allyan Magic

    At 5th level, the following Force tricks are added to your Force tricks table.
    When you take this feature, you can replace any of your Force tricks with the ones in this table.

    Trick Casting time Effect
    Healing Bonus Action You touch yourself or another creature. It recover 1 Force Die + your charisma modifier hit points.
    Guardian Aura Action Any creature you choose in your range including you gain 1 Force Die in temporary hit points.
    Bless Bonus action You gift a part of your Force to 1 creature you choose (except yourself). This creature can consume this gift to have a bonus equal to one of your Force Die to an ability check, saving throw or attack roll. The gift is lost if the creature takes a long rest.
    Invisible Hand Reaction When you see a creature failing a Dexterity or Strength saving throw or skill check, you can roll 1 Force Die and give it a bonus equal to half the amount.

    Night Spells (NightSisters)

    At 5th level, your Force tricks are totally corrupted by the NightSisters practices: all your Force tricks now deal necrotic damage.
    Moreover, the following Force tricks are added to your Force tricks table.
    When you take this feature, you can replace any of your Force tricks with the ones in this table.

    Trick Casting time Effect
    Force Drain Action You target a creature in your range, then you touch yourself or another creature. The target takes 1 Force Die + your Charisma modifier necrotic damage, and the creature you touch recover half the amount of hit points.
    Night touch Action You point at one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed a Charisma saving throw or take 1 Force Die necrotic damage. If the target is missing any of its hit points, it can't recover any hit points until the end of his next turn.
    Bane Action You point at one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed a Charisma saving throw or have a penalty equal to one of your Force Die to his attacks rolls for 1 turn.
    Cursed spirit Bonus Action You make eye contact with one organic creature you can see within range. The target must succeed a Charisma saving throw or being cursed until the end of your turn. When a cursed target die, a green spirit rise from its body and hurl to the closest creature hostile to you in 30ft, dealing 1 Force Die damage and repeating this trick on it.

    Come back

    At 9th level, you gain the following Force trick. It doesn't count against the number of Force tricks you know. This Trick need to consume 4 Force Dice to be used. Trick | Casting time | Effect -----------------------------|--------------|-------- Come back to your loved ones | 1 minute | You touch a creature that has died within the last minute. That creature returns to life with 1 hit point. This magic can’t return to life a creature that has died of old age, nor can it restore any missing body parts.

    Come back (NightSisters)

    At 9th level, you gain the following Force trick. It doesn't count against the number of Force tricks you know. This Trick need to consume 4 Force Dice to be used. Trick | Casting time | Effect -----------------------------|--------------|-------- Come back to your master | 1 minute | You touch a creature that has died within the last minute. The body rises and returns to artificial life for 1 Force Die hours. The creature is visibly corrupted and resembles an empty shell. The creature follows your orders. You can have only one of these undead creatures at a time.

    Allya Song

    At 15th level, when you have to make a death saving throw at the start of your turn, you can instead spring back to your feet, singing with a burst of powerful energy. You regain hit points equal to half your hit point maximum, and then you stand up if you so choose. Each creature of your choice that is within 30 feet of you takes force damage equal to 10 + your Charisma modifier, and is charmed until the end of the current turn.

    Night Song (NightSisters)

    At 15th level, when you have to make a death saving throw at the start of your turn, you can instead spring back to your feet, singing with a burst of cursed energy. You regain hit points equal to half your hit point maximum, and then you stand up if you so choose. Each creature of your choice that is within 30 feet of you takes necrotic damage equal to 10 + your Charisma modifier, and is frightened until the end of the current turn.
